As a zzp'er (freelancer) in the Netherlands, you pay income tax on your profit. You also pay the income-related health insurance contribution (Zvw-bijdrage). A few important amounts changed in 2026. Below you find them in one overview, with the official source for each amount.

## In short

- The self-employed deduction (zelfstandigenaftrek) is **€1,200** [1].
- The start-up deduction (startersaftrek) is **€2,123** [1].
- The SME profit exemption (mkb-winstvrijstelling) is **12.7%** [2].
- The Zvw contribution is **4.85%** on income up to **€79,409** [3]. In 2025 it was 5.26% on income up to €75,864.
- The general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting) is at most **€3,115** and the labour tax credit (arbeidskorting) is at most **€5,685** [4][5].

Sources for 2026: [3][4][5][6]. The 2025 amounts come from the Belastingdienst (the Dutch Tax Administration).

## Entrepreneur's allowance (ondernemersaftrek) in 2026

### Self-employed deduction

If you meet the hours criterion (urencriterium), you get a self-employed deduction of **€1,200** in 2026 [1]. If you have reached the state pension age (AOW-leeftijd) at the start of the year, you get half: **€600**. The self-employed deduction is lower than in 2025.

Is your profit lower than €1,200? Then your self-employed deduction cannot be higher than your profit. This limit does not apply to starters.

### Hours criterion

You meet the hours criterion if you work at least **1,225 hours** a year on your business [7]. Hours you spend on administration, finding clients and travel also count. Keep a record of your hours, because the Belastingdienst can ask for proof.

### Start-up deduction (startersaftrek)

Are you a starter, and do you meet the hours criterion? Then you get a start-up deduction of **€2,123** on top of the self-employed deduction [1]. If you have reached the state pension age, it is **€1,062**. Read the conditions on the Belastingdienst website.

## SME profit exemption

After the entrepreneur's allowance, another **12.7%** comes off your profit: the SME profit exemption [2]. You also get it if you do not meet the hours criterion.

## Deductions save you at most 37.56%

In 2026, the self-employed deduction, the start-up deduction, the SME profit exemption and other deductions save you tax at a rate of at most **37.56%** [6]. If your income is in the highest bracket (above €78,426), you pay back the difference with the top rate of 49.50%. This is called the tax rate adjustment (tariefsaanpassing).

## Box 1 tax brackets in 2026

| Taxable income | Rate below state pension age |
|---|---:|
| up to €38,883 | 35.75% |
| €38,883 to €78,426 | 37.56% |
| from €78,426 | 49.50% |

Source: [4][6]. If you have reached the state pension age, the rate in the first bracket is 17.85%.

## Zvw contribution

As a zzp'er, you pay the income-related Zvw contribution yourself. In 2026 it is **4.85%** of your taxable profit, on up to **€79,409** [3]. Do you also earn a salary from a job? Then that salary counts towards this maximum.

## Worked example: €50,000 profit

Take a zzp'er with no other income who is below the state pension age. This person meets the hours criterion and is not a starter.

| Step | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Profit | €50,000 |
| Self-employed deduction | − €1,200 |
| SME profit exemption (12.7% of €48,800) | − €6,197 |
| Taxable profit | €42,603 |
| Box 1 tax | €15,297 |
| General tax credit | − €2,291 |
| Labour tax credit | − €5,398 |
| Income tax | €7,608 |
| Zvw contribution (4.85%) | €2,066 |
| **Total** | **€9,674** |

So this zzp'er needs to set aside about **19%** of the profit. Work out your own situation with the [freelancer tax calculator](calc:zzp).

## VAT: small business scheme

Is your turnover in the Netherlands no more than **€20,000** a year? Then you can choose the small business scheme (kleineondernemersregeling, KOR) [8]. You then do not charge VAT (btw), but you also cannot reclaim VAT. Read the conditions on the Belastingdienst website.

## What should you do?

1. Calculate your tax for 2026 with the [freelancer tax calculator](calc:zzp).
2. Change your provisional assessment (voorlopige aanslag), the advance tax bill based on your expected income, if your profit is different from what you expected. You then pay every month and do not get a large bill afterwards.
3. Every month, put a fixed percentage of your turnover in a separate account.
4. Keep a record of your hours for the hours criterion.

## Bookkeeping

Good bookkeeping makes your tax return easier. You then see your profit every month, and how much you need to set aside.
