# Dutch tax credits 2026

A tax credit (heffingskorting) reduces the tax you pay, not your income. The two most important are the general tax credit (algemene heffingskorting), which everyone is entitled to, and the labour tax credit (arbeidskorting) for people who work, whether employed or self-employed. Employers usually apply them already through payroll tax.

# General tax credit 2026

- Below state pension (AOW) age: up to €3,115 with an aggregate income (verzamelinkomen) up to and including €29,736.
- Above that, the credit drops by 6.398% of income over €29,736.
- From an aggregate income of €78,427, the credit is €0.
- Above AOW age for the whole year: up to €1,556, phase-out 3.195%, also €0 from €78,427.

# Labour tax credit 2026 (below AOW age)

- Employment income up to and including €11,965: 8.324% of employment income.
- €11,966 up to and including €25,845: €996 + 31.009% of the amount over €11,965.
- €25,846 up to and including €45,592: €5,300 + 1.950% of the amount over €25,845.
- €45,593 up to and including €132,920: €5,685 − 6.510% of the amount over €45,592.
- From €132,921: €0.

Above AOW age for the whole year, the labour tax credit is at most €2,840. Reaching AOW age during 2026? The Belastingdienst then calculates your credits via the provisional assessment form.

# Worked examples

Employee with €30,000 employment income (which is also their aggregate income):

- General tax credit: €3,115 − 6.398% × €264 = €3,098
- Labour tax credit: €5,300 + 1.950% × €4,155 = €5,381
- Total credits: about €8,479

Employee with €90,000 employment income:

- General tax credit: €0 (income above €78,426)
- Labour tax credit: €5,685 − 6.510% × €44,408 = €2,794
- Total credits: about €2,794

Because of the phase-out, within the phase-out band you pay more tax on an extra euro than the bracket rate alone. See tax-brackets-2026 for the rates.

# Other tax credits

There are other credits, such as the income-related combination credit, the elderly person's credit and the young disabled person's credit. We have not checked those amounts for this guide. See belastingdienst.nl for details.
